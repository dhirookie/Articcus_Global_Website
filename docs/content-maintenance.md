# Content maintenance

The website leads with growing businesses that need clearer websites and less manual work. Overseas development support remains a secondary path. The full catalogue stays available, while the homepage groups it into three starting points.

## Where to edit

- Homepage composition: `src/features/home/home-page.tsx`; headline and product illustrations: `hero-content.tsx`.
- Service facts and indicative timelines: `src/data/catalogue.ts`.
- Detailed scopes: `src/data/service-details.ts`.
- Illustrative workflows: `src/data/solution-examples.ts`.
- Buying guides: `src/data/guides.ts`.
- Identity and primary navigation: `src/config/site.ts`.
- Shared delivery language: `src/components/content/delivery-expectations.tsx`.

## Add real evidence when it is available

Solution examples are labelled illustrations. Replace or supplement them with approved projects only when there is publishable evidence. For each case study, collect the client's permission, problem, scope, actual screenshots, delivery period, outcome and measurement method. A proposed benefit is not a measured result.

For testimonials, confirm the exact quotation and the person's name, role and publication permission. For team profiles, get approved bios and photographs. Do not invent headcount, tenure, certifications, partnerships, clients or delivery metrics.

## Pricing and promises

Pricing currently gives scope examples and cost drivers, not fabricated price ranges. Before adding prices, confirm currency, tax treatment, included work, recurring charges, expiry date and exclusions with the business owner.

Response time language is an aim, not a fabricated service-level agreement. Timelines are indicative and depend on scope, access and reviews. Confirm warranty, ownership, third-party licensing and payment terms in the signed proposal. Keep the advance payment explanation consistent across pages.

## Improve with evidence

Review enquiry completion, delivery errors and response times first. Track qualified conversations, estimates and wins in the configured enquiry management system. Website measurement deliberately has no contact details or visitor identifier, so business outcome reporting belongs in the receiving system.

Once traffic is sufficient, change one part of the enquiry journey at a time and compare qualified enquiries over a representative period. Review the guides when real customer questions reveal missing information. Publish dedicated industry pages when you can support them with specific expertise or approved examples.
